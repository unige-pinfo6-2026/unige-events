# Contributing to UNIGE Events

First of all, thank you for your interest in the UNIGE Events project! 

## 🎓 Academic Scope & Contributions Policy

This repository hosts a software engineering project developed as part of a **university curriculum (PINFO) at the University of Geneva (UNIGE)** by the members of **Group 6**.

Because this project is evaluated as part of our academic coursework:
* **No external contributions are accepted**: We are unable to merge Pull Requests or accept code contributions from individuals outside of the official Group 6 university team.
* **Educational Purpose**: The code is made open-source under the MIT License for educational purposes, academic review, and portfolio demonstration.

## 🔍 Feedback and Inquiries

While we cannot accept code contributions, we highly appreciate feedback, bug reports, or architectural questions:
* Feel free to open an **Issue** if you spot a bug or have a question about the microservices setup.
* If you are a student or researcher looking to study the repository or reuse parts of it under the MIT License, you are very welcome to fork it!

Thank you for your understanding!
